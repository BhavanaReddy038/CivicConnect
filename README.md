\# CivicConnect



\## AI-Powered Civic Complaint \& Accountability Platform



CivicConnect is a civic issue reporting and accountability platform that connects citizens, authorities, and communities through AI-powered complaint processing, authority routing, SLA management, community verification, and civic analytics.



\---



\## Project Modules



\### Person 1 — Citizen + AI Intelligence



\- Citizen reporting

\- Complaint management

\- AI classification

\- Severity detection

\- Media processing

\- Location processing



\### Person 2 — Authority + Accountability



\- Authority dashboard

\- Department management

\- Complaint assignment

\- Priority management

\- SLA tracking

\- Escalation management

\- Status management



\### Person 3 — Community Verification + Analytics



\- Community issue feed

\- Issue confirmation

\- Duplicate detection

\- Resolution verification

\- Before/after evidence

\- Heatmaps

\- Civic analytics



\---



\## Technology Stack



\- Frontend: React

\- Backend: FastAPI

\- Database: PostgreSQL / PostGIS

\- AI: AI/LLM Services

\- Cache: Redis

\- Containerization: Docker
